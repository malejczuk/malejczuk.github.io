---
title: The Studio
date: May 21, 2020
description: "My mind is unwritten music / And a million notes played at once: / A blank stave overflowing."
---

My mind is unwritten music <br>
And a million notes played at once: <br>
A blank stave overflowing. <br>
And in this chaotic emptiness <br>
Lies the melody that seeks to be found!

Not merely a blank canvas <br>
Nor a concealed masterpiece, smothered in grime; <br>
An unobserved quantum state, <br>
It is both at once! <br>
_I paint as I dust._ 

This is the studio <br>
And in the studio I am free <br>
To twist and turn and bob and weave! <br>
I would come here often <br>
But it's not often that I have the key.
