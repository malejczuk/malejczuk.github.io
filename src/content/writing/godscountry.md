Endless plains unfold beneath me. I see freedom in the cracked earth. God’s country, undamaged by the conditioned expectations of perpetual abundance.

I am from the land of conditioned expectations and perpetual abundance. It is a sick place. It is a sick place because we have abundance but we do not have relationship, and abundance without relationship is poison.

Where I am from, the abundance is tangible but the labour is abstracted. We are not foolish people. We know that abundance comes from somewhere, that it is arrived at through a process. We know that there is labour but we do not do the labour and we do not see the labour, so the labour becomes an abstraction.

As we abstract away the labour, alongside it we abstract away our gratitude. With deep knowledge and little understanding, we meet uninterrupted abundance with unconsidered expectation. This is what I mean when I say that I am from a sick place.

Endless plains unfold beneath me. I see freedom in the cracked earth. God’s country, undamaged by the conditioned expectations of perpetual abundance. This is where abundance is created; it is not a place for abstraction. The only abstraction here is God.
