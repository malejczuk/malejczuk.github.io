# Travel

I backpacked for a year around Latin America, from September 2024 to September 2025.
It was the most enriching experience of my life, and I use the word enriching literally: these travels made me extraordinarily rich (though not in the monetary sense).
I recommend such a trip to anyone.
I will fill out this travel page with more information, but for now I leave you with my itinerary.
If you have any questions, reach out to me.
