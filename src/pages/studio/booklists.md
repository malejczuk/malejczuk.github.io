## 20th Century Hispanic Literature

I am reading these books to:
1. Expose myself to key works of modern hispanic literature
2. Improve my Spanish level

The selection is quite balanced in terms of New and Old World authors, though there is a Barcelonian bias (Marsé, Laforet, Ruiz Zafón) as this is where I am living.
The booklist is ordered by the approximate difficulty of the prose.
